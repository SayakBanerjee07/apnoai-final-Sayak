About Us

Why members stay with us.

Core Fitness began in 2019 as a small training hall with one squat rack and one promise: every member gets a plan built for them, not a printout. Today we have a full strength floor, a cardio zone and a yoga studio, and our trainers still know every member by name.

Our Classes

Three ways to train at Core Fitness.

Strength Training

Free weights, racks and guided programmes for beginners and lifters alike. A trainer checks your form every session.

Cardio & HIIT

High-energy 45-minute group sessions that burn fat and build stamina. Morning and evening batches.

Yoga & Mobility

Daily classes for flexibility, recovery and stress relief. Open to all levels, mats provided.
